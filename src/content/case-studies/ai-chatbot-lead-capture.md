A nutrition and wellness coaching business had steady website traffic but no real conversion path — visitors would land, browse, and leave with no mechanism guiding them toward an actual consultation.

The chatbot guides each visitor through a short set of personalised questions, identifies their specific wellness goal, recommends a concrete next step based on their answers, and routes qualified visitors directly into a booked consultation with a specialist. Every interaction — booked or not — captures clean, structured lead data straight into the CRM, so nothing gets lost even when someone doesn't convert on the first visit.

The business now has a real, working funnel between "visitor" and "booked consultation" where there was none before.
