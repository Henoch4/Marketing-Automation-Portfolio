The client's CRM had degraded to the point where staff were manually auditing it every single day just to catch what the automation should have been catching on its own. Field mappings were broken, trigger conditions were firing incorrectly or not at all, and pipeline stages didn't reflect where deals actually were.

This was a full audit-and-rebuild, not a patch. Field mapping was corrected end to end, every trigger condition was rebuilt and tested, pipeline stages were restructured to match the real sales process, and calendar and form integrations were reconnected properly. Automated email and SMS follow-up was layered back on top once the foundation was actually reliable.

Daily manual CRM audits are gone. The system now does what a CRM is supposed to do without someone checking behind it every morning.
