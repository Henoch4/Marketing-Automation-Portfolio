A three-phase pipeline covering lead generation, outreach, and pipeline management end to end. Leads get captured through Telegram or webhook triggers, then enriched automatically via Apollo.io before a single email is written.

Outreach itself is generated per-lead by GPT-4 — genuinely personalised rather than templated with a first-name merge field — and every sequence runs through a Smart Triage layer that reads incoming replies and classifies intent. The moment a prospect actually responds, reply-triggered stop sequences halt all further automated follow-ups on that lead, so nobody gets a robotic email three days after they've already replied.

The result was over 80% less manual outreach time for the sales team, with lead quality and personalisation both going up, not down, as the manual load came off.
