Leads were arriving through forms and calendar bookings and then sitting untouched until someone remembered to follow up manually — which, in practice, meant a meaningful share of leads never heard back at all.

The build connected forms, calendar, and the CRM pipeline into one sequence: new leads get an immediate automated response the moment they come in, and continued nurture follow-up kicks in automatically if they don't book. Delivery timing was tested and tuned rather than guessed at, workflow triggers were corrected where they were firing late or not at all, and CRM updates were validated to make sure the pipeline stayed accurate as leads moved through it.

Lead engagement improved measurably, and the manual follow-up burden that used to depend on one person remembering is gone entirely.
