A boutique online retailer was handling every support function manually — payment verification, order tracking updates, and general customer queries were all routed through a small team with no automation layer underneath them at all.

The build centralised all of it into a single automation engine: automated payment verification, real-time tracking updates pushed to customers without anyone asking, AI-powered support triage that routes tickets to the right resolution path immediately, post-purchase follow-up sequences, and proactive exception management that flags problems before a customer has to complain about them.

Response times dropped sharply, and the review rate improved as a direct result of customers actually hearing back fast.
