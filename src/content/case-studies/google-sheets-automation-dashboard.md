The client was running a genuinely useful set of spreadsheet automations, but had zero visibility into whether they were actually working — checking sync status meant opening raw sheets and manually scanning rows for errors.

The dashboard surfaces everything that matters at a glance: sync success rate, rows processed, failed rows, which sheets are currently connected, and live automation status. Trend charts show whether things are getting better or worse over time, and validation logic flags failures before they compound into bigger data problems.

The client now has a real control panel for their automation stack — no more opening raw spreadsheets just to find out if something broke.
