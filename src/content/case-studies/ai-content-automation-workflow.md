The client was manually rewriting the same core content for every platform it needed to go out on, then manually publishing each version and manually tracking what had already gone out where — hours of repetitive work every single week.

Built on Make and OpenAI, the workflow pulls source inputs from a central sheet, generates platform-specific post variations automatically, and routes each output to the correct platform. An approval step is built into the flow rather than bolted on afterward, and duplicate-publish protection prevents the same content from going out twice by accident.

What used to take hours of manual work every week now runs on its own, with a human only touching the approval step.
