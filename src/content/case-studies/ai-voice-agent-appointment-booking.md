The client was losing real revenue every night — patients calling after hours, getting no answer, and simply calling the next practice on their list. There was no voicemail-to-booking pipeline, no after-hours coverage, and no way to know how many opportunities were being lost.

The system built on Twilio for call handling and GoHighLevel for the CRM and calendar layer. The voice agent answers every inbound call immediately, works through a qualification script to understand what the caller needs, checks real-time calendar availability, and books the appointment directly — all without a human touching a single call.

The practice now has full 24/7 phone coverage with zero added headcount, and every booked appointment is already qualified before it hits the calendar.
